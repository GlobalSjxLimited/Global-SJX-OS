Who we are

Global SJX Limited is an Information Technology, Management Advisory and Solutions Delivery company headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. We help organizations navigate growing complexity — providing technology solutions, engineering services, management advisory and implementation expertise that reduce operational and information risk.

Our experience spans banking and finance, insurance, manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, government and technology-enabled services.

Payment security

Strengthening card transaction security for a leading Nigerian commercial bank

Financial institution · Payment security engagement

Challenge

The organization required improved security for digital card transactions, alongside growing regulatory and customer expectations around fraud protection.

Solution

Global SJX implemented EMV 3-D Secure and card tokenization technology, integrated with the bank's existing payment infrastructure and monitored through our fraud management capability.

Result

Improved transaction security, greater visibility into fraud risk and stronger customer protection across digital card channels.

Sina Joseph

Sina Joseph is a highly accomplished professional with over 30 years of experience in banking, technology, payments and leadership. He graduated with 1st Class honors in Mathematics from the University of Benin in 1990 and holds Alumnus status from Harvard Business School, having completed the General Management Programme (GMP) alongside various other leadership programs.

As Managing Partner/CEO of Global SJX Limited, Sina has been instrumental in driving growth and innovation.
